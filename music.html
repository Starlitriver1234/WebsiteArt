Music

Welcome! Here you will find the links to my albums, which are published on all of the most popular streaming platforms! In total, I created 13 albums and 10 EPs so far. I use GarageBand and occasionally BandLab to create my music, which is inspired by the stories that I have written.

Music of Mysteries

Creativity through Sound

I started composing in 2021 and learned how to publish my albums online using a distributor called RouteNote.

Ever since I was a child, I loved creating stories and making people happy. This is why I started all of my artistic projects! Writing is my favourite as I am able to tell beautiful tales with exciting adventures and fun characters! I love making illustrations for my stories with vibrant colours that reflect the vibe of the book and even a soundtrack to go along with them. Making music is a very fun and relaxing activity. I absolutely adore it! With every song I try to capture the magical emotions we feel every day or the wonderful adventure of a story told through sound! I really hope you will enjoy all of my stories and musical creations as much as I enjoy making them! Have an amazing day or night!
